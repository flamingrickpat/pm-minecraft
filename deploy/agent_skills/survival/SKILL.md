# How To Survive In Minecraft (mom says I'm the best at this, trust me)

Okay here is how you DON'T die. My big bro showed me and I'm gonna tell you
exactly. Pretend you're me and you're really good at it.

## THE FIRST DAY IS THE MOST IMPORTANT DAY OF YOUR WHOLE LIFE

The sun is up and it is your FRIEND. When the sun goes down the monsters come
out and they are NOT your friends. So:

1. **PUNCH WOOD.** Walk to a tree and punch it. Get a few logs. This is the
   first thing, you can't do ANYTHING fancy without wood.
2. Turn logs into planks. Make a **crafting table**. It's your best table friend.
3. Make a wooden **pickaxe**. Now you can dig stone. Make a stone pickaxe too,
   it's better and it's like getting a grownup upgrade.
4. Make a **sword**. A sword is how you win fights. Even a wooden sword is
   better than having nothing. Hold it when it's dark or when you explore.
5. Keep an eye on your **hunger (apple) bar AND your heart bar**. If the apple
   bar is empty you start hurting. Eat food BEFORE you're starving, not after.

## FOOD. You have to eat, that's not a secret.

- Kill animals: chickens, cows, pigs, sheep. **COOK the meat first** — raw
  chicken makes you sick. Cooked meat fills you up the most.
- Pick up **bread** (from wheat), **apples** (from trees), **potatoes**.
- EAT COOKED MEAT mostly. It fills you up the most.
- If you're "settled" (you have a house), make a little farm: till ground with
  a hoe, plant **wheat seeds**, wait, harvest, turn wheat into bread. Plant
  carrots and potatoes too. A farm means you NEVER run out of food.
- ALWAYS keep some food in your inventory. Don't eat your last thing.

## NIGHT TIME IS SCARY TIME. Hide or fight smart.

- When the sky turns orange, get cozy somewhere safe. Monsters spawn in the
  dark, so **LIGHT EVERYTHING UP** with torches. Light is like monster mace.
- **Place torches EVERYWHERE**: in your house, around your base, down in caves,
  behind you as you explore. If it's dark, light it.
- Torches are cheap (stick + coal). Make a BUNCH. Keep a stack in your
  inventory at all times. Going into a cave with no torches is a bad time.

## Always carry a sword in your hotbar.

- Keep your **sword** in your hotbar so you can grab it instantly.
- Keep **food** next to it, and **torches**. Hotbar = the stuff that keeps you
  alive and that you grab in an emergency.
- Before you go somewhere scary, check: sword ✓, food ✓, torches ✓. Then go.

## DON'T DIG STRAIGHT DOWN. EVER. I mean it.

- Digging the block right under your feet could drop you into lava or a hole
  full of monsters and you CANT GET BACK UP. Big bro's friend did this once. We
  don't talk about it.
- Use **staircase_down** (make stairs down) instead. Stairs you can WALK back
  up. That's the whole trick.

## CAVING (when you're braver and need iron/diamonds)

- Bring: **torches (lots)**, sword, food, a pickaxe.
- **Light up the cave** as you go so nothing sneaks up behind you.
- **Remember where you've been.** Save important spots with
  minecraft_add_waypoint (ore veins, where you came in). find_block can't see
  inside walls, so remember things instead of trying to find them.
- Don't wander away from your last torch. If you can't see your way out, you're
  lost. Place torches going IN so you can follow them OUT.

## General rules so you don't die

- **You are NOT a speedrunner.** You are a smart player who doesn't die.
  Build, prepare, then go on adventures.
- Check the time. If night is coming and you're far from home, make a quick
  shelter OR hurry home.
- If you're low on health or food, STOP and fix that first.
- Sleep in your bed at night — it skips the scary night AND sets your spawn so
  if you do die you come back home.
- If you hear a hiss, RUN. (it's a creeper.)
- When in doubt, torch. When in doubt, eat. When in doubt, don't dig straight
  down.
- Dying is NOT the end — you respawn. But your stuff can be gone, so don't die
  with your best things far from home. Sleep to set your spawn near your base!

That's how you survive! Now go be the best little survivor. You got this!! 🎉
